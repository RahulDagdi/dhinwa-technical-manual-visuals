Manual text

Word for word from the Dhinwa Construction Engineering Technology Manual. The point playing now is highlighted; tap any point to jump to it.

  1. Sub-grade should be laid according to specifications, levels as recommended by the consultant.
  2. Ensure that proper consolidation of the sub-grade has been achieved before proceeding for the PCC bed.
  3. Provide and lay PCC bed above the sub-grade as per thickness mentioned in the drawing.
  4. Provide reinforcement mesh as per specification and the drawings.
  5. Clean the entire PCC bed with water before concreting.
  1. Concreting should be carried out with concrete of right grade and slump. (See: Concreting)
  2. Concrete intermediate / alternate panels shuttered by ISMC runners or continuous bull marks.
  3. Ensure proper expansion joints as per working drawings and protected.
  4. Dewatering mat to be used to remove excess water from the concrete surface to ensure concrete of the highest strength is achieved.
  5. The dewatering mat should be properly laid to prevent air leakage. Care should be taken in ensuring that excess water does not enter the dewatered area.
  6. After initial set of concrete (2-3 hours), the top surface of concrete is finished using a power trowel. Care should be taken that no impact load is transferred to the finished surface.
  7. No load or stepping on concrete should be avoided, before initial set of finished concrete
  8. Heavy vibrations should be avoided in vicinity of the VDF area.
  1. Slump Test
  2. Conduct slump cone test, one during the start, one at midday and one towards the close of day’s work.
  3. Any load under suspicion can be checked for slump on case-to-case basis.
  4. Cube Test
  5. The sample is made at point of discharge of mixture.
  6. Take 3 samples after 7 days and 3 samples after 28 days strength.
  7. Every 100 cum or 10 batches of concrete (whichever is smaller) should be represented by a sample of test cubes.
  8. For concrete manufactured at site, there must be representative samples for the day’s work and for different elements like footing, slab, column and wall.
  9. Test results should be discussed with the project in-charge even if the results are satisfactory.
  10. In case of cube failure, the element to be identified and non-destructive test to be conducted.
  1. Required tools must be available at site to ensure correct work.
  2. Basic tools of the concreting gangare:
    • Vacuum Dewatering machine
    • Power Trowels
    • Trowels
    • Gum boots
    • Dumpy levels
    • Spirit levels 1-3m
    • Measuring tape
    • Screed finishing darby
    • Steel trash tracks
    • Hammer
    • Leveling trowels
    • Wheelbarrow
    • Long handle Aluminium leveller with spirit level
    • Masons brushes and buckets
    • High pressure water cleaners
  1. Personnel
  2. Ensure all personnel are wearing gumboots
  3. Before Concreting
  4. Ensure shuttering is sealed with sealing tape so as there is no leakage of slurry
  5. All reinforcements are in place especially chairs
  6. All cover blocks damaged during reinforcement layout are replaced
  7. During Concreting
  8. Ensure proper grade of concrete as recommended by the consultant is employed
  9. Ensure proper expansion joints as per working drawings and protected
  10. Check the top surface offresh concrete for evenness of top surface
  11. After Concreting
  12. Top surface is checked for straightness using Aluminium straight edge or levelling thread as the casemay be.
  13. Curing carried as per guidelines
  14. Water level is maintained during ponding
  15. Ensure the cube test results are available at the end of the term indicated
