Manual text

Word for word from the Dhinwa Construction Engineering Technology Manual. The point playing now is highlighted; tap any point to jump to it.

  1. Refer latest duly approved “Good for Construction” drawings.
  2. If the fabrication works are done at site, ensure proper electrical connections and proper working platform.
  3. All the joints should be properly welded from either sides.
  4. Excess weld should be grinded off, but ensure too much of grinding should be avoided on welded joints as it may tend to weaken the joints.
  5. After grinding the edges and all corners should be finished off with metal paste.
  6. Allow the metal paste to completely dry and sand the parts to perfection.
  7. Avoid chamfered or filleted edges; ensure joints to be in perfect plane and perpendiculars.
  8. Apply one coat of metal primer with brush before fixing.
  9. Ensure rosettes are placed in position before fixing the grills or railings.
  10. Painting 2 coats enamel for railings after the completion of external painting works.
  11. Anchor bolts to be used instead of coach screw, for fixing railing to concrete/ blockwork.
  12. Rosette detail to be given for all holdfasts going into wall/ floor.
  13. Rosette should be painted keeping it away from the wall and after drying pushed to fix in place using spot adhesive.
  14. All mortar spillage stuck to MS works shall be cleared after the day’s work. It is good practice to cover the grill with a plastic cover while the plastering is in progress.
